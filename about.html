About Movie Seat Booking

Welcome to Movie Seat Booking, your go-to application for reserving seats at your favorite movie theater! Our mission is to provide you with a seamless and enjoyable movie-going experience from the comfort of your own home.

With Movie Seat Booking, you can easily browse available movies, view showtimes, and select your preferred seats. We aim to make the seat selection process as easy and intuitive as possible.

Here's what you can do with our application:

  • Browse Movies: See what's playing at the theater.
  • Select Seats: Choose the perfect seats for you and your friends.
  • Check Availability: See which seats are available and which are already taken.
  • View Prices: Know the cost of your seats before you book.
  • Secure Your Seats: Reserve your seats quickly and easily.
